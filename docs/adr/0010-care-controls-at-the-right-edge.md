# Care controls stacked at the band's right edge

Amends ADR-0008, which put the dim glyph controls beside the pet. Following the pet, they moved about the band all the time, and the owner found them floating around. Now `♥`, `∴` and `✦` stand one above the other at the band's right edge and stay put while the pet walks about. Everything else in ADR-0008 holds: dim one-glyph buttons, the same hotkeys, no text reply. Three stacked controls fit the band's three text rows in a terminal; the desktop app may draw the band a little taller.

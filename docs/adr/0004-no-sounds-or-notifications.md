# No sounds, toasts or notifications; only the emote

The pet must keep the owner company without pulling attention from work. It never plays sounds, raises toasts or notifications, or writes to the status line. It expresses itself through its behaviour and a short dim emote beside it (at most about 12 characters, changing at most once every ten seconds). The only unasked text outside the emote is the adoption hint in the basket. Exact numbers are available on request with `/pet status`.

Considered: chiptune sounds (rejected), toasts when hungry (rejected), no text at all (too limiting; the emote stays).
